## Overview

I built Lattice Matrix as an interactive environment for exploring mathematical structures that are difficult to understand through disconnected notes and tables alone.

The application collects focused research workbenches for physical constants, fine-structure scaling, atomic and electromagnetic relationships, cross-lattice derivations, binary transformations, and exact sequence searches. Each workbench can define its own mathematical assumptions while sharing the same catalog, visual language, data conventions, and validation principles.

The product foundation and current workbench collection are complete and usable. Research development remains active as I refine derivations, challenge candidate patterns, add new investigations, and turn the strongest results into detailed journal articles.
