## Overview

I built Nexus Wave as a connected environment for developing and understanding music projects.

Rather than replacing a digital audio workstation, Nexus Wave operates around the creative process. It brings albums, tracks, lyrics, audio analysis, pronunciation, melodic interpretation, project documentation, and audio-reactive visualization into one application.

The core product is complete and usable. Development remains active as I expand its analysis models, visual engines, mobile experience, and experimental systems.
